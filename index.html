      The Story of OVEN

      MELLOW & CRUMB began on Vijay Path in Mansarovar with a clear promise: to serve Jaipur a warm neighborhood espresso bar and luxury patisserie that is 100% eggless and pure vegetarian.

      Every morning at 10 AM, our kitchen pairs freshly pulled Arabica coffee with small-batch Belgian chocolate ganaches, saffron-infused sponges, and savoury cafe plates designed for unhurried conversations.
